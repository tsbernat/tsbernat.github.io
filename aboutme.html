Tatum Bernat

Marine Evolutionary Ecologist

Hello! My name is Tatum and I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Graduate Group in Ecology at UC Davis. Broadly, I am drawn to eco-evolutionary questions about how organisms respond to environmental disturbances. More specifically, I am interested in the ways in which genomes can confer phenotypic resiliency and the implications this has for ecological interactions.

To this end, I am currently working with Dr. Andrew Whitehead to reveal the genomic architecture that allows some species of killifish to exhibit extreme osmoregulatory phenotypic plasticity (euryhalinity) on fast timescales. I am using transcriptomics and comparative genomics to understand how closely related killifish species have lost this plasticity, in order to uncover how it is acheieved in the first place.

Let's learn about science together!
